## What PROVED means here

A row is PROVED when `verdicts.tsv` records VERIFIED: every Frama-C/WP goal of the stated function contract, including the runtime-error goals, is discharged by the provers on the real, unmodified translation unit (the annotations are full-line ACSL comments; stripping them gives the pristine source). - Faithfulness: strip-check of every annotated unit against the pristine baseline (cycle log records the re-runs).

- Non-vacuity: refutation probes and attacks are recorded in the verdict notes.
