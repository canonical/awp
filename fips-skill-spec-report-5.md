## 2. New findings

### Blocking

**B-1. The §3 FIPS ledger has two homes, and the one §6 specifies is the wrong one (§4.1 file tree, §6 files-table rows for `fips-crosswalk.md` and for the `frama-c-monitoring/SKILL.md:858-865` sentence, §7.1 "The coverage ledger" vs "The producer").**
- What the two documents are today. `frama-c-launch/templates/rmf-crosswalk.md` is the hand-filled launch-side join: §1 "Asset crosswalk (RMF supporting asset <-> translation unit)" (`:31`), §2 "Scenario crosswalk" with the `Supporting asset -> TU(s)` column (`:39,45`), §3 "Verification feedback into the RMF Monitor step (filled by the frama-c-monitoring §8e close)" (`:70-79`); `rmf-feedback.md:54` says "Fill **crosswalk §3** (one row per scenario touched)". It has no ledger. The ledger is §3 of `frama-c-monitoring/templates/crosswalk-template.md:42-79`, the "FORMAT CONTRACT (authoritative)" of `ledger-ratchet.sh:13`, and it exists only inside the generated `PREFIX-crosswalk.md` (`crosswalk-gen.sh:216-225`; `crosswalk-generation.md:3-5`, "generated, not hand-written"). `rmf-sl/references/crosswalk-format.md:8-9` is a thin pointer to `crosswalk-template.md`, not to `frama-c-launch`'s template.
- What the spec says. §7.1: "So `fips-crosswalk.md` §3 is a FIPS ledger … (a `# ledger: fips` marker line in the crosswalk selects it)"; §6: "`skills/frama-c-launch/templates/fips-crosswalk.md` (with the §3 FIPS ledger of §7.1)" and, for `frama-c-monitoring/SKILL.md:858-865`, "or the FIPS ledger of `frama-c-launch/templates/fips-crosswalk.md` §3"; §4.1: "`crosswalk-format.md` — the schema of the join table; the filled template is `frama-c-launch/templates/fips-crosswalk.md`". Then §7.1 "The producer": "`bin/crosswalk-gen.sh` is the only writer of `PREFIX-crosswalk.md` … §3 from `PREFIX-acsl/fips-ledger.tsv`". The ledger cannot be both the hand-filled §3 of a `frama-c-launch` template and the generated §3 of `PREFIX-crosswalk.md`.
- Consequences. The ledger table is written into a template that neither `crosswalk-gen.sh` nor `ledger-ratchet.sh` reads; `fips-feedback.md`, "turns verdicts into evidence keyed by `RQ#`" on the `rmf-feedback.md` pattern, has no §3 to fill or collides with the ledger; `crosswalk-template.md` keeps a STRIDE-only §3 (`asset × {S,T,R,I,D,E}`, `:42,75-79`), so the generated FIPS §3 and the ratchet's second row form conform to no schema while the ratchet's header calls that file authoritative; the `crosswalk-gen.sh` boilerplate "Lifecycle per template §3.1; ratchet per §3.2" (`:218`) points at a template that does not describe the FIPS table.
- Fix. (1) §7.1 "The coverage ledger": "the §3 ledger of the generated `PREFIX-crosswalk.md` is, under `fips`, a FIPS ledger: one row per `VO#` …". (2) Files table: add `skills/frama-c-monitoring/templates/crosswalk-template.md` §3: a FIPS form next to §3.3 (`VO#`, `AR#`, class, cell; the `# ledger: fips` marker; the same §3.1 lifecycle and §3.2 ratchet rule), the row for line 10 becoming "lines 10 and §3". (3) `fips-crosswalk.md` mirrors `rmf-crosswalk.md`: §1 `MOD#`/`SV#` ↔ translation unit, §2 the `VO#` ranking by `P1`–`P3` with the `SV# -> TU(s)` column, §3 the feedback table into F5 that `fips-feedback.md` fills; drop "(with the §3 FIPS ledger of §7.1)". (4) §4.1: `crosswalk-format.md` is the thin pointer to `crosswalk-template.md` with the FIPS tag bindings (`VO#` for the §3 rows, `VO#` for the §4 join, `RR#` for §5), like `rmf-sl`'s `:22-37`. (5) The `SKILL.md:858-865` sentence: "or the FIPS ledger of `crosswalk-template.md` §3 (`# ledger: fips`)".
