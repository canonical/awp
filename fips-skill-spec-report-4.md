# Review of `fips-skill-spec.md`, revision 5

**Verdict.** Not ready as written, for two reasons; otherwise close. Every finding of report 3 is resolved, the renaming to F0–F7 is consistent everywhere (every cross-reference in §1, §4.4–§4.6, §5 and §10 points to the right rule, and the `rmf-sl` rules are always named "its rule 0/5/6"), and every PDF, `auto-wp.py`, `ledger-ratchet.sh`, template, test and document citation I re-checked holds. Two things block: (1) the F-C1 canary ("F4 heading removed") cannot fail "with that check's ID and no other" under the spec's own home-line definition, since removing the F4 heading leaves every `VO#` without a home line (F-C0) and every `RQ#` uncovered (F-C6) — one sentence fixes it; (2) the FIPS coverage ledger has no producer: `bin/crosswalk-gen.sh` is the only thing that writes `PREFIX-crosswalk.md` and its §3 ledger, it knows `--kind rmf|ebios` only and exits 2 without a `PREFIX-rmf-report.md` or `-ebios-report.md`, and `frama-c-monitoring/SKILL.md` step 10 runs it at campaign close ("Do NOT hand-write the crosswalk"). The spec teaches `ledger-ratchet.sh` the FIPS row form but never mentions `crosswalk-gen.sh`, so in a `fips` project the campaign close errors out and the ratchet has nothing to ratchet. This needs a design decision, not a wording fix. The rest is a test list that misses six hard-coded counts, and minor items.

## 2. New findings

### Blocking

**B-1. The F-C1 canary trips F-C0 and F-C6 as well (§4.4 rule F0, §5 F-C0, F-C1, F-C6, canary table, §8 T9).**
